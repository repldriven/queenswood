# Banks: gap analysis

Subject: [banks.md](../banks.md) — Banks.

## Verdict

**Fail.**

The tenant boundary the TDD describes holds: every bank carries a
fountain-allocated sort code behind a unique index, the create flow
runs every foundational write inside one `store/transact` and rolls
the lot back on any anomaly, the identity-provider call precedes the
FDB write, the org party, the ledger chart, the own-funds house
account and the tier bindings are all minted as described, and
`client_id == bank-id`. The verdict fails on what has moved around
that core:

- A bank created with any currency other than GBP is rejected by the
  own-funds step and rolled back, although the API offers three
  currencies and the TDD says "per currency" throughout (F1).
- The brick the TDD calls synchronous is a command processor reached
  over the bus from a different service, with its reads split into a
  sibling brick (F2).
- The tier the TDD says is never stored is stored, and the tier
  change the TDD and both PRDs say does not exist is an endpoint
  (F3).
- The create flow has grown five steps, a second caller under the
  `user` gate, and a ninth ledger row that none of the documents list
  (F4, F5, F6).
- Nothing exercises the all-or-nothing claim, the multi-currency
  create, or an unknown tier at creation (Missing evidence).

The verdict flips to pass when F1 is settled in either direction with
a scenario, the Architecture, create-flow, record and limitations
sections describe the code, the two PRDs describe the tier and status
transitions that exist, and a rollback test guards the claim the TDD
is built on.

## What was examined

- The subject TDD, and the sibling TDDs it cites: authentication,
  chart-of-accounts, parties, cash-account-products, cash-accounts and
  policy-evaluation, plus the interest TDD for the 5100 / 2400 legs.
- The `bank` brick: interface, core, domain, store, changelog,
  commands and system files, and its three test namespaces.
- The `bank-query` brick: interface, core and store.
- The `api` base: the bank routes, commands, components and examples,
  the onboarding handler and route, and the tier route.
- The `Bank` proto, the record-type metadata for the `banks` store,
  the five Avro schemas under `schemas/banks`, the general-ledger seed
  and the own-funds product template under `resources`.
- The system YAML for the bank processor and dispatcher, and the
  api-service and operational-processors-service application files
  that host them.
- The `policy`, `ledger-account`, `cash-account-product`, `scheduler`
  and `fdb` interfaces the flow composes, and the `identity-provider`
  interface of the `mono` dependency at the tag pinned under
  `deps/mono`.
- The API scenario suite's `banks`, `me`, `ledger-accounts` and
  `payments` folders.
- The PRDs that cite the TDD or its subject: onboarding, platform and
  policies.

Nothing was executed. Every finding below was traced in source, and
the ones that predict runtime behaviour say so.

## What matches

- `allocate-sort-code` draws from a global counter formatted `%06d`
  inside the create transaction, `Bank_by_sort_code` is a unique index
  in the record-type metadata, and the sort-code fountain scenario
  proves two banks get distinct codes and an opened account's BBAN
  carries its bank's.
- `new-bank` wraps every step in one `store/transact` threaded through
  `let-nom>`, so any anomaly rolls back the bank, the party, the ledger
  rows, the house accounts and the bindings together.
- `create-service-account` runs before `store/create`, with
  `client_id == bank-id` and the status-derived audience the handler
  forwards; the secret it mints is discarded and the handler calls
  `rotate-secret` after the reply, exactly as the TDD's step 5 and the
  authentication TDD say.
- `domain/new-bank` runs the `:bank-action-create` capability check
  with the requested status, mints a `bnk.` id, and the effective
  policies come from `get-effective-policies txn {}` unless `opts`
  overrides them.
- The org party is `:party-type-organization` with display-name equal
  to the bank name; one `LedgerAccount` per seed row per currency is
  opened; the house account is a `:product-type-sub-ledger-own-funds`
  product drafted, published with `effective-from` today, and an
  ordinary `CashAccount` on the org party carrying the bank's sort
  code.
- Tier bindings target `{:kind {:bank {:bank-id <id>}}}`, and
  `get-effective-policies` returns platform policies plus those bound
  to the bank.
- `change-status` is guarded on test or live, rejects a flip to the
  same status, and calls `update-service-account-audience` before the
  FDB write.
- The enriched read walks the org party and the cash accounts with
  balances and resolved `:gl-code`, sets `:client-id` to the bank id,
  and omits the seeded ledger accounts.
- `get-bank-by-sort-code` attributes an inbound to its bank by the
  BBAN's first six digits; the code uses it on the unmatched-suspense
  path, which the inbound-unmatched-suspense scenario exercises.
- Nothing relays or consumes the `banks` changelog: no relay-runners
  entry names the store.

## Findings

Severity is High where a stated guarantee is broken, Medium where the
design and the code disagree in a way a reader would act on, Low where
the document is incomplete.

### F1. High — a bank cannot be created in any currency but GBP

The TDD fans `currencies` across the ledger chart and the own-funds
house account "per currency", and the API's `CreateBankRequest`
accepts a non-empty set drawn from the `Currency` enum, which holds
EUR, GBP and USD. The house-account step creates the own-funds product
from the template `tpl.…04`, and that template's `allowed-currencies`
is `[GBP]`. Product creation checks the requested currency against the
template and rejects `:cash-account-product/currency-not-allowed`, so
a create naming EUR or USD is rejected at step 9 and the whole bank
rolls back. Traced, not executed. The route documents no such
response, and no scenario creates a bank with more than one currency.
Evidence:
[own-funds.yml](/components/resources/resources/cash-account-product-templates/own-funds.yml),
`ensure-currency-allowed` in the cash-account-product brick's
`domain.clj`, and `Currency` in the api base's shared components.

Fix: widen the template's allowed currencies to match the request
schema — the house product is multi-currency. That follows from how
the chart identifies an account: by its role and its currency, not by
its number. A bank holding EUR, GBP and USD holds three own-funds
accounts all coded 3100, one full ledger chart per currency, and
sub-ledger cash-accounts that fan out the same way. Record the
decision in the TDD and add a scenario that pins a multi-currency
create.

### F2. High — the Architecture section describes a brick that is gone

The TDD says `bank` has a "synchronous interface — no command
processing, no event consumers", and its diagram wires HTTP straight
into `new-bank`. The brick is a `BankProcessor` dispatching
`create-bank`, `change-bank-tier` and `change-bank-status` off the
`banks-command` channel; the api base sends those commands through a
dispatcher, waits on `banks-command-response`, and never requires the
`bank` interface. The processor is hosted by the operational
processors service, which wires its own Keycloak identity-provider for
the purpose. Reads live in `bank-query`, which the TDD does not name,
and `new-bank` returns a flat `{:bank :membership}` rather than the
enriched view: the handler loads the view from `bank-query` after
`rotate-secret`. Evidence:
[commands.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/commands.clj),
[bank.yml](/components/resources/resources/system/bank.yml), and the
api base's bank `commands.clj`. The authentication TDD already
describes the round trip; this one contradicts it.

Fix: rewrite Architecture around the command path and the query split,
redraw the diagram with the dispatcher and the processor service in
it, and cite the processor-bricks TDD and ADR-0021 for the shape.

### F3. High — the tier is stored and changeable, and three documents say not

The TDD says twice that `tier` "isn't even stored on the record" and
lists "No tier change after creation" as its first limitation. The
`Bank` proto carries `tier` as field 10, `domain/new-bank` stamps it,
and `change-tier` rebinds a bank onto a new tier in one transaction:
it drops every binding whose policy carries a `tier` label, binds the
new tier's policies, persists `:tier`, and rejects `:bank/invalid-status`
or `:bank/unknown-tier`. The API exposes it as
`POST /v1/banks/{bank-id}/change-tier` and two scenarios cover it. The
onboarding PRD lists tier transitions under both non-goals and open
questions, the platform PRD says there is no flow "between tiers
post-creation, nor between live and test status", and the policies
PRD says the tier choice is permanent. Evidence:
[bank.proto](/components/schema/resources/schemas/banks/bank.proto),
`change-tier` in
[core.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/core.clj),
[onboarding.md](../../prd/onboarding.md),
[platform.md](../../prd/platform.md) and
[policies.md](../../prd/policies.md).

Fix: add `:tier` to the record, give the tier change its own section
alongside the status change with both rejections, and rewrite the
three PRDs so a tenant can be moved between tiers and between test and
live, in product terms.

### F4. Medium — the create flow has grown steps the TDD does not list

The eleven-step list is missing what the code now does before,
between and after those steps:

- The identity-provider is required, and its absence is rejected
  `:bank/missing-identity-provider` before anything else. The TDD
  says the client is created "when `opts` carries" one.
- When `:membership` is supplied, a sole-membership check runs before
  any write and rejects `:membership/already-exists`, and the owner
  membership is created last, in the same transaction.
- `domain/new-bank` rejects `:onboarding/company-not-active` when a
  `:company-binding` is supplied whose status is not active, and the
  record carries the binding.
- `scheduler/seed-jobs` seeds the bank's default scheduled jobs after
  the tier bindings.
- Tier policies are resolved at the bind step, not as step 2, and
  there is no enrich step.
- The platform policies resolved at step 1 are threaded into every
  foundational write, so a tier that denies the ledger-account or
  product capability per bank still bootstraps. The TDD says they are
  "used for the create capability check".

Evidence: `new-bank` in
[core.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/core.clj)
and
[domain.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/domain.clj).

Fix: rewrite the step list and the record shape against the code, and
say which steps are conditional on which `opts` key.

### F5. Medium — a second caller creates banks under the `user` gate

The diagram labels the entry point "HTTP create-bank admin-only", and
the last limitation says the create is admin-only by convention. The
onboarding handler under `POST /v1/onboarding/me`, gated on `user`,
sends the same `create-bank` command with fixed defaults, test status,
tier `micro` and GBP, a company binding snapshotted from a registry
lookup, and an owner membership. The onboarding PRD lists self-service
tenant signup as a non-goal and an open question, and the onboarding
TDD still says both paths converge at `organization/new-organization`.
Evidence: the api base's onboarding
[handlers.clj](/bases/api/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/api/onboarding/handlers.clj),
[onboarding.md](../../prd/onboarding.md) and the
[onboarding TDD](../onboarding.md).

Fix: name both callers and the defaults the onboarding path fixes,
restate the limitation as "the processor checks a capability, not a
principal", and reconcile the onboarding PRD and TDD.

### F6. Medium — the seeded chart has nine rows and a different 2400

The TDD's table, and the same table in the chart-of-accounts TDD,
list eight rows with 2400 interest payable as a detail account, and
the chart-of-accounts TDD says interest expense is "not seeded today".
The seed has nine rows: 5100 interest expense is seeded as an expense
detail account, and 2400 is a control account. The interest TDD's
accrual leg posts DR 5100 / CR 2400, so the ninth row is load-bearing.
Evidence:
[general-ledger.edn](/components/resources/resources/ledgers/general-ledger.edn)
and [chart-of-accounts.md](../chart-of-accounts.md).

Fix: correct both tables, or keep the table in the chart-of-accounts
TDD only and have this one link to it, so the two cannot drift again.

### F7. Medium — an unknown tier is rejected on change and accepted on create

`change-tier` rejects `:bank/unknown-tier` when the tier resolves to
no policies, with the comment that a typo "must not silently strip all
tier bindings". `new-bank` has no such guard: `bind-tier-policies`
binds whatever `get-policies-by-tier` returns, so a mistyped tier
creates a bank stamped with that tier, bound to nothing, and governed
by the platform policies alone. The API requires `tier` on the request
but the Avro schema and the core accept nil, and neither the TDD nor a
scenario says what a nil or unmatched tier means. Traced, not
executed. Evidence: `bind-tier-policies` in
[core.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/core.clj).

Fix: reject an unmatched tier at creation in `domain.clj` with the
same kind, state the nil-tier rule, and add a scenario. While there,
guard the tier lookup's anomaly: `when-let` treats an anomaly as a
policy list, and `new-binding` does not assert the policy exists.

### F8. Medium — the envelope moved, and a tier change is a status change

The TDD says a `BankChangelog` record is "the one store still writing
a bespoke changelog shape rather than the shared `ChangelogEvent`
envelope". No such record exists in the proto; `store/save` writes a
`ChangelogEvent` carrying a `bank-status-changed` Avro payload, keyed
for dedup on `bank-id:status-after`. `change-tier` calls the same
`store/save` with `status-before` equal to `status-after`, so a tier
change is recorded as a status change with no tier fields, and its
dedup key collides with any earlier transition to that status. Nothing
relays the store today, so the collision is latent. Evidence:
[changelog.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/changelog.clj)
and `change-tier` in
[core.clj](/components/bank/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/bank/core.clj).

Fix: correct the TDD sentence, give a tier change its own event name
and payload, and key dedup on something a tier change and a status
change cannot share.

### F9. Low — the response shape and where it is built

The TDD shows the enriched read as `{:bank {…} :client-secret "…"}`
with the secret beside the bank. The handler returns the bank map with
`:client-secret` inside it, and the view now carries `:tier` and
`:company-binding` when set. `CreateBankResponse` omits `:tier`
although the body carries it, which the change-tier scenario asserts
on the 201. Evidence: `bank-with-secret` in the api base's bank
`commands.clj` and
[components.clj](/bases/api/src/com/repldriven/queenswood/api/bank/components.clj).

Fix: show the shape the handler returns, name `bank-query` as where
the enrichment lives, and add `:tier` to `CreateBankResponse`.

### F10. Low — the identity-provider call inside a retried transaction

The TDD says an identity-provider failure aborts the transaction
cleanly, and stops there. The authentication TDD now records the other
direction: `create-service-account` sits inside a transaction FDB may
re-run on conflict, and a retry mints a second client that nothing
removes. This TDD's Architecture section is where a reader looks for
the boundary rule, and it does not mention it.

Fix: add the limitation here, or link the authentication TDD's entry
from the Architecture paragraph.

### F11. Low — the preamble narrates the rename

The block quote opening the document says what the entity used to be
called, which issue renamed it, and what file the document used to be.
The writing-docs recipe asks that a document not narrate what it used
to say.

Fix: drop the block quote and let the Objective stand.

## Missing evidence

- No test injects a failure mid-flow and asserts that nothing remains:
  no bank, no party, no ledger rows, no house account, no bindings. The
  all-or-nothing guarantee is the TDD's headline and rests on
  `store/transact` alone.
- No test creates a bank with more than one currency (F1), with an
  unmatched or nil tier (F7), or without an identity-provider.
- The seeded chart is asserted by embedding three rows in the
  ledger-accounts list scenario; nothing asserts the full nine rows per
  currency, or the class of 2400 and 5100 (F6).
- The changelog is tested only through its pure builder; no test reads
  back the entry `store/save` writes, or the entry a tier change
  leaves (F8).
- The company-binding gate is covered at the domain level only; no
  scenario onboards against an inactive company, and the
  `CompanyNotActive` example the route advertises has no test behind
  it.
- `get-bank-by-sort-code` has no direct test; it is reached through
  one inbound-payment scenario.

## Recommended fixes

In order:

1. Settle F1 in the template or the request schema, and add the
   multi-currency scenario.
2. Add a rollback test to the `bank` brick and the unmatched-tier
   guard at creation (F7).
3. Rewrite the TDD's Architecture, create flow, record, response
   shape, chart and limitations against the code (F2, F3, F4, F5, F6,
   F8, F9, F10, F11).
4. Reconcile the onboarding, platform and policies PRDs with the tier
   and status transitions and the self-service path (F3, F5), and fix
   the onboarding TDD's stale operation name.
5. Give a tier change its own changelog event before anything relays
   the store (F8).
