Account

A GBP savings account, on the liability side of the bank's books.

Interest

5.00% a year on the first £5,000 and nothing above it.

Earned daily and paid monthly on the day the account opened.

Term

12 months from opening. Then the account stays open and the whole balance earns 1.50%.

Limits

Up to 3 withdrawals a tax year from 6 April, and up to £50,000.00 paid in a day. The balance can't go below £0.00.

Rewards

£25.00 when the account opens.

Available

New accounts open on this version from 1 Nov 2026.
